# SCRIPT — long-night-ep2

**Voice:** am_onyx (Kokoro), speed 1.05

---

## Line 1 — beat01 (Frame 1)

    In the long dark, light is ammo. And Cole is running low.

## Line 2 — beat02 (Frame 2)

    Matches: twenty-three. Lamp oil: four liters. Flares: four. Batteries: two.

## Line 3 — beat03 (Frame 3)

    His nearest neighbor is Ruth, a retired nurse, three miles up the creek. Her chimney hasn't smoked in two days.

## Line 4 — beat04 (Frame 4)

    Cole straps on a headlamp. Bear goes first. Bear always goes first.

## Line 5 — beat05 (Frame 5)

    Halfway there, the headlamp starts to dim. Cole didn't touch it. The batteries are brand new.

## Line 6 — beat06 (Frame 6)

    Rule two: they drink light. And electric light goes first.

## Line 7 — beat07 (Frame 7)

    Ruth's cabin is dark. But through the window there's a tiny glow. A circle of candles on the floor. And Ruth, sitting in the middle. Alive.

## Line 8 — beat08 (Frame 8)

    Three days, she whispers. They've been at the window for three days. Don't let the candles go out.

## Line 9 — beat09 (Frame 9)

    Cole counts. Four candles left. Each one already half gone.

## Line 10 — beat10 (Frame 10)

    Then Bear whines. Behind them, one candle flame bends sideways. There's no wind.

## Line 11 — beat11 (Frame 11)

    And it goes out.
