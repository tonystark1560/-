One transaction. Seven thousand bitcoin. Forty million dollars. Gone in a single click.

May 7th, 2019. Binance, the biggest crypto exchange on Earth. Millions of users. Billions in coins.

Most of those coins sleep in cold wallets. Offline. Unreachable. But some live in the hot wallet, online, ready for withdrawals.

At 17:15, the hot wallet sends out one withdrawal. Seven thousand and seventy-four bitcoin.

About two percent of all the bitcoin Binance holds. In one move.

The security system doesn't stop it. The transaction looks completely normal.

Binance freezes every deposit and every withdrawal. For millions of users, the money is suddenly locked.

Then the statement. Hackers got user API keys, two-factor codes, and maybe more.
