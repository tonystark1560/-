# Proposal templates (Upwork / Freelancer)

Rules that get replies:
- **The first 2 lines decide everything**: the client sees only them in the list.
  Start with their problem, not "Hello, I am…".
- Mention a detail from their job post so it's obviously not copy-paste.
- Short: 80–150 words. Ask one question at the end.
- Attach the most relevant portfolio link.

---

## 1. Telegram / AI chatbot

> Hi [Name], you need a Telegram bot that [their task, e.g. "answers customer
> questions about your menu and takes orders"]. I've built exactly this kind of
> bot. Here's a working example with AI answers and conversation memory:
> [link to telegram-ai-bot]
>
> My plan for your bot:
> 1. [Feature from their post]
> 2. [Feature from their post]
> 3. Deploy it so it runs 24/7, plus a short guide for you
>
> I can show a first working version in [2] days.
>
> Quick question: [e.g. "Should the bot answer only from your FAQ, or also
> handle general questions?"]
>
> [Your name]

## 2. Web scraping / data collection

> Hi [Name], I can collect [data type] from [site] into [Excel/CSV/Sheets]
> with all the fields you listed: [field 1, field 2, field 3].
>
> Here's a similar scraper I built. It goes through all pages and exports
> clean data: [link to web-scraper] (sample output attached).
>
> To give you an exact quote: roughly how many [products/pages] are there, and
> is this a one-time export or should it run regularly?
>
> I can send you a free sample of the first 20 rows before you pay for the
> full job.
>
> [Your name]

## 3. Automation / integration (Zapier, Make, n8n, scripts)

> Hi [Name], so the goal is that [process from their post, e.g. "new form
> submissions automatically appear in Google Sheets and you get a Telegram
> notification"], with no manual copying.
>
> I'd do it like this: [1–2 sentences of the approach]. It's usually [1–2] days
> of work, and I'll record a short video showing how it works.
>
> One question before I start: [question about their tools/access].
>
> [Your name]

## 4. Landing page / website

> Hi [Name], I'll build a clean, mobile-friendly [landing page / website] for
> [their business], focused on [their goal, e.g. "getting people to book a call"].
>
> You'll get a first version to review within [2] days, then we polish it
> together. Unlimited small text/color changes are included.
>
> Do you already have a logo, texts and photos, or should I prepare
> placeholder content?
>
> [Your name]

## Message after you get the job

> Thanks for choosing me! To start, I need:
> 1. [access / data / examples]
> 2. [answers to open questions]
>
> I'll send you the first working version by [date]. If anything changes in the
> meantime, just message me here.

## Asking for a review after delivery

> Glad it works well for you! If you're happy with the result, a short review
> would help me a lot. And if you need changes later, I'm here.
