## Platforms to register on (in order)

1. **Fiverr**: clients come to you through gigs. Easiest start with no reviews.
2. **Upwork**: biggest market, but proposals cost "Connects". Check whether your
   country is supported before registering.
3. **Contra**: 0% commission, good for a portfolio.
4. **Freelancer.com**: lots of small bot/scraping jobs.
5. **Kwork / FL.ru**: Russian-speaking clients, quick first orders.

Get paid only through the platform until a client has paid you at least once.
Never pay "to unlock jobs" and never send money to a client.
