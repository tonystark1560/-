# The Binance Heist · Part 6: Stop the Blockchain

**Channel:** Crypto Heists · **Format:** 9:16 Shorts/TikTok/Reels

## Сценарий

The hacker starts moving the BNB out to other blockchains. Ethereum. Polygon. Avalanche.

Then Binance does something almost unthinkable. It asks the validators to stop the entire blockchain.

BNB Chain halts. No blocks. No transactions. Everything frozen.

Most of the loot, around four hundred and thirty million dollars, is trapped in the hacker's wallet. It can't move.

But about a hundred million had already escaped to other chains.

About a week later, a hard fork patches the hole. The bridge is fixed.

Critics ask: if one company can stop a blockchain, how decentralized is it really?

Two heists. One almost rewrote history. One printed money from nothing.

In 2019, the rollback was rejected. In 2022, the chain was stopped. Which would you choose? Follow for the next heist.
