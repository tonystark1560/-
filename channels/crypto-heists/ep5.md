# The Binance Heist · Part 5: Money From Nothing

**Channel:** Crypto Heists · **Format:** 9:16 Shorts/TikTok/Reels

## Сценарий

October 6th, 2022. A new thief. And this one doesn't steal money. He prints it.

Binance has its own blockchain, BNB Chain. And a bridge that moves coins between two of its chains.

The bridge works like a machine. Show it a proof that coins were sent, and it releases coins on the other side.

The hacker finds a flaw in how the bridge checks those proofs.

He forges a proof. The bridge believes it. And mints one million BNB. Out of nothing.

Then he does it again. Another million.

Two million BNB. On paper, more than five hundred and sixty million dollars.

Now he has to get it out before anyone notices. Part 6.
