# The AI Gold Rush: Who Will Be the Next to Fall?

## Описание для YouTube

> Every gold rush has winners and a graveyard. Railways in the 1840s. The dot-com boom in 1999. And now, artificial intelligence. What history tells us about which AI companies will survive, and which will become the next Pets.com.

## Сценарий

### 0:00 — Intro

[ЭКРАН: толпа старателей бежит к сияющей горе.]

Every gold rush in history follows the same pattern. A new technology appears. Everyone realizes it will change the world. Money floods in.

[ЭКРАН: гора рушится, по склону катятся люди.]

And then, most of the people who rushed in lose everything.

[ЭКРАН: серверы и чипы светятся, надпись «AI».]

Right now, we're in the middle of the biggest tech gold rush in decades: artificial intelligence. So let's look at what happened last time, and ask who's next to fall.

### 0:45 — Railway mania

[ЭКРАН: Британия 1840-х, паровозы, газеты с котировками.]

Let's go back to Britain in the 1840s. A new technology was transforming the world: the railway.

[ЭКРАН: карта, на ней прорастают сотни линий, многие ведут в никуда.]

Investors went crazy. Hundreds of railway companies were created. Parliament approved thousands of miles of new lines. Ordinary people put their savings into railway shares.

[ЭКРАН: график акций взлетает и падает.]

Then, in 1846 and 1847, the bubble burst. Share prices crashed. Many companies went under. Many investors were ruined.

[ЭКРАН: годы спустя по рельсам едут поезда.]

But here's the twist: the railways got built. And they did change the world. The technology was real. Most of the companies weren't.

### 2:15 — The dot-com boom

[ЭКРАН: 1995 год, модем пищит, браузер загружается.]

Fast forward to the 1990s. The new technology was the internet.

[ЭКРАН: 1995, первые торги Netscape, счётчик взлетает.]

When Netscape, the company behind one of the first popular web browsers, went public in 1995, its stock soared on day one. Wall Street got the message: the internet is the future.

[ЭКРАН: логотипы «.com» сыплются как конфетти.]

By the late 1990s, almost any company with ".com" in its name could raise millions. Profits? Didn't matter. Revenue? Optional. Growth was everything.

[ЭКРАН: пёс-кукла из носка (Pets.com) в рекламе Супербоула.]

Pets.com sold pet food online. It had a famous sock puppet mascot and a Super Bowl ad. It went public in February 2000.

[ЭКРАН: грузовики Webvan, склады размером со стадион.]

Webvan wanted to deliver groceries to your door. It raised hundreds of millions of dollars and built gigantic warehouses.

### 3:45 — The crash

[ЭКРАН: март 2000, график Nasdaq на вершине.]

In March 2000, the tech-heavy Nasdaq index hit its peak.

[ЭКРАН: график падает со скалы, табло краснеет.]

Over the next two and a half years, it fell nearly 80 percent.

[ЭКРАН: Pets.com: табличка «CLOSED», кукла лежит в коробке.]

Pets.com shut down in November 2000, just nine months after going public.

[ЭКРАН: склады Webvan пустеют.]

Webvan went bankrupt in 2001. Hundreds of dot-com companies disappeared. Trillions of dollars in stock market value were wiped out.

### 5:00 — Who survived

[ЭКРАН: маленькая коробка с улыбкой выбирается из руин.]

But not everyone died. Amazon's stock crashed more than 90 percent. Many people thought it was finished. It survived, and became one of the most valuable companies on Earth.

[ЭКРАН: Google: маленький офис, 1998.]

Google was founded in 1998, right in the middle of the bubble, and came out the other side as the king of search.

[ЭКРАН: годы спустя: онлайн-доставка продуктов и кормов для животных — нормальная вещь.]

And the funny part? Selling pet food and delivering groceries online turned out to be great ideas. They were just too early, too expensive, and built on hype instead of solid business.

[ЭКРАН: крупно фраза.]

The lesson from both railways and dot-coms is the same. The technology wins. Most of the early companies lose.

### 6:15 — The AI boom

[ЭКРАН: 2022–2026, чат-бот отвечает на вопросы, мир ахает.]

Now look at today. When AI chatbots exploded into the mainstream, the whole tech industry turned toward AI almost overnight.

[ЭКРАН: огромные дата-центры, ряды серверов до горизонта.]

Big tech companies are spending staggering amounts on data centers and chips. Startups with "AI" in their pitch raise money at huge valuations, sometimes before they have real revenue.

[ЭКРАН: стартап-логотипы с надписью «AI» сыплются как конфетти, как в 1999.]

Sound familiar?

### 7:45 — Warning signs

[ЭКРАН: тикеры акций краснеют.]

In October 2026, AI and chip stocks had a brutal week, with reports of huge combined losses as investors worried about the cost of all that building.

[ЭКРАН: три карточки с предупреждающими знаками.]

There are some classic warning signs to watch for.

One: companies spending far more than they earn, assuming the money will come later.

Two: startups whose whole product is a thin layer on top of someone else's AI model. If the big model adds that feature, the startup is gone.

[ЭКРАН: стартап-«обёртка» вокруг большого ИИ, большой ИИ поглощает его.]

Three: huge valuations based on "users" who might not stick around, like we just saw with Character.AI.

### 9:00 — Who falls first

[ЭКРАН: две двери: над одной «Amazon», над другой «Pets.com».]

So who survives? If history is any guide, it's companies that own something hard to copy: unique data, real customers who pay, or the infrastructure everyone else depends on.

[ЭКРАН: горизонт, рассвет над дата-центрами.]

AI will almost certainly change the world, just like the railways and the internet did. But that doesn't mean every AI company will be around to see it.

[ЭКРАН: финальная заставка RISE&RUIN.]

Which AI company do you think will be the Amazon of this era, and which one will be the next Pets.com? Tell me in the comments, and subscribe so you don't miss the next fall.
