# FTX: From Crypto King to Prison

**Канал:** Rise & Ruin · **Формат:** 16:9 · **Длина:** около 10 минут (примерно 1450 слов)
**Превью:** взъерошенный парень в шортах и футболке, на голове падающая корона, график криптовалюты обрывается, «$8B GONE».

## Описание для YouTube

> He was the golden boy of crypto: billionaire at 29, Super Bowl ads, stadium naming rights, and a promise to give it all away. Then, in about a week, FTX collapsed and billions in customer money were missing. The rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried.

## Сценарий

### 0:00 — Ten days

[ЭКРАН: офис в Багамах, солнце, бассейн. Счётчик состояния: $16,000,000,000.]

At the start of November 2022, Sam Bankman-Fried was one of the richest people in crypto, worth billions on paper.

[ЭКРАН: счётчик падает до нуля за несколько секунд.]

About ten days later, his company was bankrupt, his fortune was basically gone, and billions of dollars of customer money had disappeared.

[ЭКРАН: заголовок «FTX FILES FOR BANKRUPTCY».]

This is the story of FTX.

### 1:00 — The math kid

[ЭКРАН: аудитория MIT, парень с кудрявыми волосами решает задачи.]

Sam Bankman-Fried, or SBF, grew up in California. Both of his parents were law professors at Stanford. He studied physics at MIT.

[ЭКРАН: трейдинговый зал с экранами.]

After college, he joined Jane Street, an elite Wall Street trading firm. He was good at it. But he wanted more.

[ЭКРАН: весы с надписями «earn» и «give».]

SBF said he believed in an idea called "earning to give": make as much money as possible, then give it away to good causes. It became a huge part of his public image.

### 2:30 — Alameda and the arbitrage

[ЭКРАН: карта мира, цены биткоина в США и Японии разные.]

In 2017, he started a crypto trading firm called Alameda Research.

One of its early strategies was famous. Bitcoin was trading for more in Japan than in the US. So in theory, you could buy in America, sell in Japan, and pocket the difference.

[ЭКРАН: стрелки денег летят через океан.]

Moving money that way wasn't easy. But Alameda found ways to do it and made serious money.

That early success gave SBF a reputation as a genius trader. And it gave him the money to build something bigger.

### 3:45 — FTX takes over

[ЭКРАН: появляется логотип-заглушка «FTX», вокруг растут цифры пользователей.]

In 2019, he launched FTX, a crypto exchange where people could buy, sell and trade.

It grew insanely fast. In early 2022, FTX was valued at 32 billion dollars.

[ЭКРАН: стадион с надписью «FTX Arena», реклама на Супербоуле.]

FTX put its name on the Miami Heat's arena. It ran a Super Bowl ad. It signed deals with celebrities like Tom Brady.

[ЭКРАН: SBF в шортах в Конгрессе, политики жмут ему руку.]

SBF became the face of "responsible crypto." He testified in Congress. He donated to politicians. He played video games during investor calls, and people loved it. It made him seem real.

### 5:00 — The secret pipe

[ЭКРАН: два здания: «FTX» и «Alameda». Между ними скрытая подземная труба.]

But there was a problem hidden at the center of everything.

FTX and Alameda were supposed to be separate companies. In reality, they were deeply connected. Prosecutors later showed that customer money deposited on FTX had been moved to Alameda and used for trading, investments, real estate and political donations.

[ЭКРАН: монеты утекают по трубе.]

And a lot of Alameda's "assets" were in FTT, a token created by FTX itself. A coin whose value mostly depended on everyone believing in FTX.

It was a house built on its own shadow.

### 6:15 — The bank run

[ЭКРАН: 2 ноября 2022, новость на сайте CoinDesk.]

On November 2, 2022, the crypto news site CoinDesk published a leaked balance sheet showing how much of Alameda's money was tied up in FTT.

[ЭКРАН: CEO Binance нажимает кнопку «SELL FTT».]

Days later, the head of rival exchange Binance announced he would sell his company's FTT tokens.

[ЭКРАН: толпа у дверей банка, но двери цифровые, кнопки «Withdraw».]

Panic. Customers rushed to withdraw billions of dollars in a matter of days. But FTX didn't have the money.

[ЭКРАН: рукопожатие Binance и FTX, затем Binance отдёргивает руку.]

Binance briefly agreed to buy FTX. Then, after looking at the books, it walked away.

[ЭКРАН: печать «CHAPTER 11», 11 ноября 2022.]

On November 11, FTX filed for bankruptcy. The man brought in to clean up the mess, John Ray III, who had also overseen the end of Enron, said he had never seen such a complete failure of corporate controls.

### 7:30 — Prison

[ЭКРАН: Багамы, полиция, декабрь 2022.]

In December 2022, Bankman-Fried was arrested in the Bahamas and sent to the United States.

[ЭКРАН: зал суда, свидетели по очереди выходят к трибуне.]

At trial, his closest colleagues, including former Alameda CEO Caroline Ellison, testified against him.

[ЭКРАН: «GUILTY» на 7 пунктах, затем «25 YEARS».]

In November 2023, he was found guilty on all seven counts of fraud and conspiracy. In March 2024, he was sentenced to 25 years in prison.

### 8:45 — The strange ending

[ЭКРАН: бухгалтеры в пустом офисе собирают остатки.]

And here's the strange twist. The bankruptcy team recovered a lot of money, from investments FTX had made with customer funds, including a stake in an AI company that later soared in value.

[ЭКРАН: график возвратов клиентам.]

The bankruptcy plan aimed to repay customers in full, based on the value of their accounts at the time of the collapse. Even though crypto prices later went much higher, which many customers felt was unfair.

[ЭКРАН: падающая корона катится и останавливается.]

FTX shows how quickly trust can turn into a bank run. And that when someone tells you they're the "responsible" one, it's worth asking who's checking.
