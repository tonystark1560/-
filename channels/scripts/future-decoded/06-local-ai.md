## Сценарий

### 0:00 — AI without internet

[ЭКРАН: самолёт в небе, внутри маскот работает с ИИ на ноутбуке. Значок Wi-Fi перечёркнут.]

You're on a plane. No Wi-Fi. And you're still chatting with an AI assistant that summarizes your documents, writes emails and helps with code.

[ЭКРАН: облако улетает, чип внутри ноутбука загорается.]

No cloud. No servers. The AI is running right there, on your laptop. That's local AI. And in 2026, it went from a nerdy hobby to something regular people can actually use.

[ЭКРАН: маскот с табличкой «let's decode».]

So what is it, why does it matter, and should you try it? Let's decode it.

### 0:50 — Cloud AI vs local AI

[ЭКРАН: схема: телефон → интернет → огромный дата-центр → ответ обратно.]

When you use most AI chatbots today, here's what happens. You type a message. It travels over the internet to a giant data center. A massive AI model running on very expensive chips processes it. And the answer travels back to you.

[ЭКРАН: схема: ноутбук → чип внутри → ответ.]

With local AI, the model lives on your own device. Your message never leaves your computer. The "thinking" happens on your own chip.

[ЭКРАН: сравнение размеров: огромный кит (облачная модель) и дельфин (локальная).]

The catch: the models you run locally are usually smaller than the giant cloud ones. But they've gotten surprisingly good. And that's the whole story.

### 2:00 — Why now?

[ЭКРАН: график: качество маленьких моделей растёт год за годом.]

So why is local AI suddenly a thing? Three reasons.

[ЭКРАН: причина 1: модели «худеют», но остаются умными.]

One: models got smarter and smaller. Researchers found ways to pack more ability into fewer parameters. A small model today can beat a much bigger model from just a couple of years ago.

[ЭКРАН: причина 2: коробки «open model» распаковываются, их скачивают по всему миру.]

Two: open models. More and more AI labs release "open-weight" models that anyone can download and run. That includes big tech companies and research labs around the world. Some newer open models are designed specifically to run on regular laptops.

[ЭКРАН: причина 3: чипы с отдельным блоком «AI», новые ноутбуки.]

Three: hardware. Laptop and phone chips now come with parts designed specifically for AI. Some computers are even marketed as built to run advanced AI models locally. Manufacturers are betting that "AI on your device" will be a big reason to buy a new computer.

### 3:30 — Why people want it

[ЭКРАН: замок на ноутбуке, данные остаются внутри.]

Why would you want AI on your own device? Here are the big reasons.

Privacy. Your data never leaves your machine. For lawyers, doctors, companies with secret projects, or anyone who just doesn't want their private thoughts on someone else's server, that's huge.

[ЭКРАН: самолёт, горная хижина, метро без сети.]

Offline. It works on a plane, in a cabin in the woods, or anywhere with bad internet.

[ЭКРАН: счётчик подписки обнуляется.]

Cost. Once you have the hardware, running a local model is basically free. No monthly subscription, no per-message fees.

[ЭКРАН: ответ появляется мгновенно, без индикатора загрузки.]

Speed for small tasks. No waiting for the internet round trip. For quick things like autocomplete or summarizing a short note, local can feel instant.

[ЭКРАН: пользователь настраивает модель как конструктор.]

And control. You choose the model. You decide how it behaves. Nobody can change it or take it away from you overnight.

[ЭКРАН: «Real uses», карточки появляются по кругу.]

So what do people actually use local AI for? Here are some real examples.

[ЭКРАН: юрист, перед ним папки с грифом «confidential».]

A lawyer summarizing confidential contracts without uploading them anywhere. A doctor's office drafting notes without sending patient details to an outside company.

[ЭКРАН: программист в самолёте, ИИ подсказывает код.]

A programmer getting code suggestions on a long flight. A student using an AI tutor in a place with unreliable internet.

[ЭКРАН: журнал-дневник, ИИ помогает разобрать мысли, замок на обложке.]

People writing private journals and using AI to reflect on them, knowing nobody else will ever read those words.

[ЭКРАН: маленькая компания встраивает модель в свой продукт.]

And small companies building AI features into their own products, without paying a cloud provider for every single request.

[ЭКРАН: телефон, фотографии сортируются, текст переводится без сети.]

In fact, you might already be using local AI without realizing it. Many phones now run small AI models on the device for things like sorting photos, transcribing voice notes, translating text or suggesting replies.

### 5:00 — What you need

[ЭКРАН: ноутбук разбирается на части, подсвечиваются память и чип.]

So what do you need to try it?

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Memory», шкала 8 → 16 → 32 → 64 GB.]

The most important thing is memory. AI models need to fit in your computer's memory to run well. A basic laptop can run small models. A more powerful machine with lots of memory can run much smarter ones.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Chip»: процессор с AI-блоком.]

A modern chip helps a lot. Recent laptops, especially ones with powerful built-in graphics or dedicated AI hardware, run models much faster.

[ЭКРАН: интерфейс простого приложения: «Download model → Chat».]

And then you need an app. There are now free, user-friendly apps that let you download an open model with one click and start chatting, no coding needed. You pick a model, wait for the download, and you're in.

[ЭКРАН: маскот пробует, ноутбук слегка нагревается, вентилятор крутится.]

Tip: start with a small model. If it runs smoothly, try a bigger one. If your laptop fans start screaming, you've found your limit.

### 6:15 — The trade-offs

[ЭКРАН: весы: на одной чаше маленький чип, на другой огромное облако.]

Of course, there are trade-offs.

[ЭКРАН: сложная задача: маленькая модель спотыкается, большая справляется.]

Local models are usually less capable than the best cloud models. For really hard tasks like complex reasoning, long documents or advanced coding, the giant cloud models still win.

[ЭКРАН: батарея разряжается быстрее.]

Running AI uses a lot of power. On a laptop, it can drain your battery faster and heat things up.

[ЭКРАН: дорогой ноутбук с ценником.]

The best experience needs good hardware, which isn't cheap.

[ЭКРАН: открытая модель без «предохранителей».]

And open models can be modified by anyone, which is great for freedom, but it also means safety features can be removed. That's one reason governments and companies debate how open AI should be.

[ЭКРАН: гибрид: простое на ноутбуке, сложное в облаке.]

For most people, the future is probably hybrid: small, private tasks on your device, and big, heavy tasks in the cloud.

[ЭКРАН: «Quick recap», карточки складываются в стопку.]

Quick recap. Local AI runs on your own device instead of a company's servers. It's possible now because models got smaller and smarter, open models are widely available, and new chips are built for AI. The big benefits are privacy, offline use, low cost and control. The trade-offs are less raw power, battery drain and the need for decent hardware.

### 7:30 — The future

[ЭКРАН: телефон, часы, очки, автомобиль, все со светящимся чипом.]

Where is this going? Every year, local models get smarter and chips get more powerful. Your phone, your laptop, maybe even your car and your glasses will have capable AI built in.

[ЭКРАН: телефон 2026 года превращается в телефон через несколько лет, «IQ» растёт.]

[ЭКРАН: две дороги: «AI rented from big companies» и «AI you own».]

There's also a bigger question here: who owns AI? If the most powerful AI only runs in a few giant data centers, a few companies control it. If capable AI runs on billions of personal devices, power is spread out. Many people in tech see local, open AI as a way to keep AI from becoming something you can only rent.

[ЭКРАН: маскот выбирает дорогу «you own».]

That debate is going to shape the next decade.

The AI in your pocket is getting smarter every month. And the more it runs on your own device, the more control you have over it.
