## Сценарий

### 0:00 — Sci-fi becomes policy

[ЭКРАН: старая книга фантастики, робот на обложке. Книга превращается в правительственную папку.]

Ten years ago, "superintelligence" was something you read about in science fiction or in philosophy books. Today, it's on the agenda at the White House.

[ЭКРАН: документ с печатью, подпись появляется сама.]

According to a report by Semafor, a White House task force focused on superintelligence has adopted its charter. That starts a 120-day countdown to a report on the risks of the most powerful AI systems.

[ЭКРАН: маскот смотрит на таймер «120 days».]

So what is superintelligence? Are we actually close to it? And why is the government suddenly preparing for it? Let's decode it.

### 0:50 — What is superintelligence?

[ЭКРАН: лестница: «narrow AI» → «AGI» → «superintelligence».]

Let's start with three levels.

[ЭКРАН: ступень 1: калькулятор, шахматная программа, фильтр спама.]

Level one: narrow AI. That's AI that's great at one specific thing. A chess program. A spam filter. A face recognition system. It's superhuman at its task, but useless outside of it.

[ЭКРАН: ступень 2: робот, который учится, пишет, программирует, рисует.]

Level two: artificial general intelligence, or AGI. That's AI that can do most intellectual tasks as well as a human: learn new things, reason, plan, write, code, across almost any area. There's no single agreed definition, and people argue a lot about where the line is.

[ЭКРАН: ступень 3: светящаяся фигура выше облаков.]

Level three: superintelligence. AI that's far smarter than the smartest humans in almost every area. Not just faster. Better at science, strategy, persuasion, invention.

[ЭКРАН: аналогия: муравей смотрит на человека.]

Some researchers describe it like this: the gap between a superintelligence and us could be like the gap between us and an ant. That's why it gets people's attention.

### 2:15 — How close are we?

[ЭКРАН: график возможностей ИИ: медленно, потом резко вверх.]

Now, the honest answer to "how close are we?" is: nobody knows.

[ЭКРАН: тесты, которые ИИ проходит: экзамены, олимпиады по программированию.]

What we do know is that AI has improved shockingly fast. In just a few years, AI models went from struggling with basic math to passing tough professional exams, solving hard coding challenges and helping scientists with research.

[ЭКРАН: две группы экспертов спорят.]

Some leaders of AI companies say systems that match or beat humans at most tasks could arrive within years, not decades. Other experts are more skeptical. They point out that today's AI still makes silly mistakes, struggles with long-term planning and doesn't really understand the physical world.

[ЭКРАН: весы неопределённости.]

But here's the thing governments are thinking about: even if there's only a chance it happens soon, the stakes are so high that it's worth preparing for. Just like you buy insurance for your house, even though you don't expect it to burn down.

### 3:45 — Why governments care now

[ЭКРАН: карта мира, флаги стран, между ними линии соперничества.]

So why is the government paying attention now? A few reasons.

[ЭКРАН: причина 1: «National security», щит.]

One: national security. Very powerful AI could be used for cyberattacks, for designing dangerous weapons, or for military planning. Governments don't want to be surprised.

[ЭКРАН: причина 2: «The race», две бегущие фигуры с флагами.]

Two: the race with other countries, especially China. The US wants to stay ahead in AI. But leading the race also means understanding the risks of what you're building.

[ЭКРАН: причина 3: «Economy», фабрики, офисы, роботы.]

Three: the economy. If AI can do most work better than humans, it could change jobs and the economy faster than any technology in history. Governments need to think about what that means for workers.

[ЭКРАН: причина 4: «Control», руль, выскальзывающий из рук.]

And four: control. Some researchers worry about a scenario where an AI system becomes so capable that humans struggle to understand or control what it's doing. That's often called the "alignment problem": how do you make sure a powerful AI actually does what we want, and keeps doing it?

### 5:15 — The task force

[ЭКРАН: стол, вокруг него силуэты чиновников, экспертов, военных.]

So what is this task force? A task force is a group set up to focus on one specific problem, usually bringing together people from different parts of the government.

[ЭКРАН: устав (charter) открывается, на нём пункты.]

Adopting a charter means it now has an official mission and rules for how it works. And the most concrete part is that 120-day window for a report on frontier AI risks.

[ЭКРАН: пирамида моделей, верхушка светится: «frontier».]

"Frontier AI" means the most powerful, cutting-edge AI models: the ones at the very top of what's possible today. These are the systems built by only a handful of companies, using enormous amounts of computing power.

[ЭКРАН: таймер 120 дней, листаются страницы календаря.]

So in roughly four months, we should expect a report on what the biggest dangers are, and possibly recommendations for what to do about them.

[ЭКРАН: «What's already been tried», таймлайн прошлых шагов.]

This isn't the first time governments have tried to get a grip on advanced AI. In 2023, world leaders and AI companies met at an AI Safety Summit in the UK. Several countries set up government AI safety institutes to test powerful models. And leading AI companies signed voluntary commitments to test their models for dangerous capabilities before releasing them.

[ЭКРАН: подписи на бумаге, некоторые бледнеют.]

But voluntary means voluntary. Critics say promises from companies aren't enough when the stakes are this high, while others worry that heavy rules could slow down innovation or hand the lead to other countries.

[ЭКРАН: канат: «safety» тянет в одну сторону, «speed» в другую.]

That tension, between moving fast and staying safe, is exactly what a task force like this has to navigate.

### 6:30 — The risks they'll study

[ЭКРАН: четыре карточки-риска появляются по кругу.]

What kind of risks are we talking about? Based on what AI safety researchers usually focus on, the report will likely look at a few big areas.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Misuse».]

Misuse: people using powerful AI to do harm, like cyberattacks or helping create dangerous biological or chemical weapons.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Accidents».]

Accidents: AI systems doing something harmful that nobody intended, because of bugs, bad goals or unexpected behavior.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Concentration of power».]

Concentration of power: a small number of companies or governments controlling the most powerful technology on Earth.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Loss of control».]

And loss of control: AI systems acting independently in ways humans can't easily stop. This sounds like a movie plot, but serious researchers and AI companies themselves now study it.

### 7:45 — What happens next

[ЭКРАН: развилка: «rules», «testing», «cooperation».]

So what might come out of this? Possibly rules requiring AI companies to test their most powerful models for dangerous abilities before release. Possibly closer cooperation between the government and AI labs. Possibly new limits on who can access the most advanced chips and models.

[ЭКРАН: маскот смотрит на горизонт, где встаёт солнце-чип.]

Or, the report could end up on a shelf, like many government reports do. We'll be watching.

[ЭКРАН: «Quick recap», карточки складываются в стопку.]

Quick recap. Superintelligence means AI far smarter than humans in almost every area. Nobody knows how close we are, but progress has been fast enough that governments are preparing. A White House task force has reportedly adopted its charter and started a 120-day clock for a report on the biggest risks of frontier AI: misuse, accidents, concentration of power and loss of control. What comes out of it could shape how the most powerful AI is built and tested.

[ЭКРАН: крупно фраза.]

But the bigger message is already clear. Five years ago, superintelligence was science fiction. Today, it's a government meeting. Whether or not it arrives soon, the people in charge are no longer treating it as a joke.
