# AI Is Coming for These Jobs First

## Сценарий

### 0:00 — The question everyone's asking

[ЭКРАН: маскот работает за столом, за его спиной выглядывает робот.]

Is AI coming for your job? It's one of the most searched questions about AI. And in 2026, it doesn't feel theoretical anymore.

[ЭКРАН: счётчик сокращений тикает, заголовки о реструктуризации.]

Companies keep announcing layoffs, and more of them mention AI as part of the reason. At the same time, many companies say they're hiring for new AI-related roles.

[ЭКРАН: маскот с табличкой «let's decode».]

So who's actually at risk? And what can you do about it? Let's decode it, without the panic and without the hype.

### 0:50 — Why some jobs go first

[ЭКРАН: профессия разбирается на задачи-кубики.]

Here's the first key idea: AI doesn't replace jobs. It replaces tasks. Every job is a bundle of tasks. Some of them AI can do, and some it can't.

[ЭКРАН: кубики окрашиваются: зелёные (легко автоматизировать) и серые (сложно).]

The tasks AI is best at today have a few things in common. They happen on a screen. They follow clear patterns. They involve reading, writing, summarizing or sorting information. And there's a lot of past data showing how they're done.

[ЭКРАН: работа с большинством зелёных кубиков подсвечивается красным.]

If most of your job is made of those kinds of tasks, your job is more exposed. If most of it involves physical work, unpredictable situations, or deep human relationships, it's much less exposed, at least for now.

[ЭКРАН: неожиданный поворот: офисные профессии краснеют сильнее, чем ручной труд.]

And that's a big twist compared to past automation. Robots in factories mostly replaced physical work. This wave of AI hits office and knowledge work first.

### 2:15 — The most exposed jobs

[ЭКРАН: карточки профессий выстраиваются в ряд.]

So let's look at the kinds of jobs AI is changing first.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Customer support»: чат с ботом, очередь клиентов.]

Customer support. AI chatbots and voice agents can now handle a large share of common questions: order tracking, password resets, simple billing issues. Human agents increasingly handle the hard, emotional cases.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Data entry & admin»: стопки форм сами заполняют таблицы.]

Data entry and admin work. Moving information from documents into systems, processing forms, scheduling. That's exactly what AI and automation tools are designed to do.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Writing & content»: тексты пишутся сами.]

Basic writing and content: product descriptions, simple marketing copy, routine reports. AI can produce a first draft in seconds. Writers who survive are the ones with taste, voice and real expertise.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Translation»: два языка, стрелка между ними.]

Translation. AI translation has become remarkably good for everyday text. Human translators increasingly focus on legal, medical, literary and high-stakes work.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Junior coding»: код пишется сам, тесты зеленеют.]

And entry-level coding. AI coding tools can write a lot of routine code. That's making some companies rethink how many junior developers they need, which is a real worry for people starting their careers.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Research & analysis»: графики, отчёты.]

Also on the list: parts of research, paralegal work, bookkeeping and some kinds of graphic design. Not the whole job, but big chunks of it.

### 4:15 — Changed vs gone

[ЭКРАН: один маскот, за ним три тени работают одновременно.]

Now here's the important part. "Changed" doesn't always mean "gone."

When ATMs arrived, many people predicted bank tellers would disappear. Instead, for years, banks opened more branches and tellers shifted to customer service and sales. Spreadsheets didn't kill accounting. They changed what accountants do all day.

[ЭКРАН: человек с ИИ работает как трое.]

With AI, the most common change so far is that one person with AI can do the work that used to take more people. That can mean fewer hires in some roles. It can also mean the same people get more done and companies grow.

[ЭКРАН: новые карточки профессий появляются: «AI trainer», «AI ops», «prompt engineer», «AI safety».]

And new jobs appear. People who build, check, manage and improve AI systems. People who use AI to start businesses that weren't possible before.

[ЭКРАН: весы: «jobs lost» и «jobs created», стрелка колеблется.]

Honest answer: nobody knows yet whether AI will create more jobs than it destroys overall. But history suggests the transition is the painful part, especially for people who don't adapt.

[ЭКРАН: лестница карьеры, нижние ступеньки исчезают.]

There's one group that experts worry about most: people just starting their careers. A lot of entry-level work, like research, drafting, simple coding and data cleanup, is exactly what AI does well. That's how young people used to learn on the job.

[ЭКРАН: молодой специалист стоит у лестницы без нижних ступеней.]

If companies stop hiring juniors because AI handles those tasks, how will the next generation get the experience to become seniors? That's a real, unsolved problem, and some companies are already rethinking how they train new employees.

[ЭКРАН: на оставшейся лестнице появляются новые ступени с иконкой ИИ.]

The good news for young people: they're often the fastest to learn new AI tools. Showing up to a job already knowing how to work with AI can be a big advantage.

### 5:30 — The safest skills

[ЭКРАН: три сияющих карточки.]

So which skills are hardest to automate? Let's look at three.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «People»: рукопожатие, медсестра, учитель.]

One: working with people. Caring for patients, teaching kids, negotiating a deal, managing a team, comforting someone. AI can help with these, but people still want people.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Judgment»: весы, человек принимает решение.]

Two: judgment and responsibility. Someone still has to decide what to do and take responsibility when it goes wrong. AI can suggest. Humans sign off.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Hands»: электрик, сантехник, медик.]

Three: skilled physical work in messy, real-world environments. Electricians, plumbers, nurses, mechanics. Robots are improving, but fixing a leak under a sink in an old house is still incredibly hard for a machine.

[ЭКРАН: бонусная карточка «Using AI»: маскот управляет роботами.]

And a bonus skill that might matter most: knowing how to use AI well. Not just asking it questions, but knowing what to trust, what to check and how to combine it with your own expertise.

### 6:45 — What you can do now

[ЭКРАН: чек-лист из пяти пунктов.]

So what can you actually do? Here are five practical moves.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 1: человек открывает ИИ-инструмент.]

One: use AI in your current job, starting this week. Find one boring task and try doing it with AI. The people who learn first have a real advantage.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 2: задачи на кубиках, человек отмечает свои.]

Two: list your tasks. Which ones could AI do? Which ones need you? Then try to spend more of your time on the second kind.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 3: человек становится «тем, кого спрашивают».]

Three: become the AI person on your team. Companies need people who understand both the work and the tools.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 4: связи между людьми, нетворкинг.]

Four: invest in relationships. Your network, your reputation and the trust people have in you are things AI can't copy.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 5: копилка «emergency fund».]

And five: build a financial cushion. If your industry gets disrupted, a few months of savings gives you time to learn and switch instead of panicking.

[ЭКРАН: «Quick recap», карточки складываются в стопку.]

Quick recap. AI replaces tasks, not whole jobs, and it's hitting screen-based office work first: support, admin, basic writing, translation and junior coding. Changed doesn't always mean gone, and new roles are appearing. The hardest skills to automate are working with people, judgment and skilled physical work. And the best defense is to start using AI yourself, now.

### 8:00 — The real threat

[ЭКРАН: два работника: один игнорирует ИИ, другой использует.]

Here's the uncomfortable truth. For most people, the near-term threat isn't a robot taking your desk. It's someone who uses AI well doing your job faster and cheaper than you.

[ЭКРАН: крупно фраза.]

AI probably won't take your job. Someone using AI might.
