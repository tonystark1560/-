### 0:00 — Lazy money

[ЭКРАН: маскот лежит в гамаке на куче монет, монеты тоже спят.]

Here's an uncomfortable question: how much is your bank paying you to keep your money there?

[ЭКРАН: карточка банка: процент «0.0…%» мигает красным.]

If you have a regular savings account at one of the biggest banks, there's a good chance the answer is: almost nothing. A tiny fraction of a percent.

[ЭКРАН: вторая карточка: «High-Yield» с процентом в разы больше.]

Meanwhile, high-yield savings accounts at many other banks pay several times more. Sometimes ten times more, or more. On the exact same dollars.

[ЭКРАН: маскот с табличкой «let's unlock it».]

So why are millions of people leaving money on the table? And should you switch? Let's unlock it.

### 0:50 — How savings interest works

[ЭКРАН: монетка в банке, рядом вырастает маленькая монетка-процент.]

First, a quick refresher. When you put money in a savings account, the bank pays you interest. Basically, a small fee for letting them use your money, since banks lend it out or invest it.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «APY = Annual Percentage Yield».]

Savings rates are usually shown as APY: annual percentage yield. That's how much your money grows in a year, including compound interest.

[ЭКРАН: снежный ком катится и растёт.]

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. Your money grows a little, then the bigger amount grows a little more, and so on.

[ЭКРАН: калькулятор: $10,000 при крошечной ставке и при высокой.]

Here's a simple example. Say you have $10,000 in savings. At a rate of a fraction of a percent, you might earn something like a few dollars to a few tens of dollars in a year. At a high-yield rate a few percent higher, you could earn hundreds of dollars a year. Same money. Same safety. Very different result.

### 2:00 — Why big banks pay so little

[ЭКРАН: огромное здание банка с колоннами, внутри кассиры, охрана, банкоматы.]

So why do big traditional banks pay so little?

One reason is cost. They have thousands of branches, lots of staff and big buildings. All of that is expensive.

[ЭКРАН: люди сидят на месте, на них табличка «we don't switch».]

The other big reason: they don't have to pay more. Many people keep their savings at the same bank as their checking account out of convenience, and rarely move it. Banks know that. Why pay more if customers stay anyway?

[ЭКРАН: стрелки: деньги клиента → банк зарабатывает больше → клиенту крошки.]

Meanwhile, banks can lend that money out or invest it at much higher rates. The gap between what they earn on your money and what they pay you is part of their profit.

[ЭКРАН: маскот с поднятой бровью.]

It's not illegal. It's not a scam. But it's not exactly in your favor either.

### 3:15 — What is a HYSA?

[ЭКРАН: карточка «High-Yield Savings Account», на ней галочки.]

A high-yield savings account, or HYSA, is simply a savings account that pays a much higher interest rate than the national average.

[ЭКРАН: ноутбук и телефон вместо здания банка.]

Most are offered by online banks, or online divisions of bigger banks. Without thousands of branches to pay for, they can pass more of the interest to you.

[ЭКРАН: деньги свободно перемещаются туда-обратно.]

It works just like a normal savings account. You can deposit and withdraw money. It's not an investment in the stock market. Your balance doesn't go down when the market crashes.

[ЭКРАН: стрелка процента движется вверх-вниз вместе со ставкой ФРС.]

One important thing: the rate is variable. It usually moves with the Federal Reserve's interest rate decisions. When the Fed raises rates, HYSA rates tend to go up. When the Fed cuts rates, they tend to go down. So the rate you see today isn't guaranteed forever.

### 4:15 — Is it safe?

[ЭКРАН: щит с надписью «FDIC», под ним монеты.]

The question everyone asks: is it safe to put money in an online bank I've never heard of?

[ЭКРАН: карточка «FDIC insured: $250,000».]

The key is insurance. At US banks, look for FDIC insurance. At credit unions, look for NCUA insurance. These government-backed programs protect your deposits, usually up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per account ownership category, if the bank fails.

[ЭКРАН: лупа над логотипом «Member FDIC» на сайте.]

That's the same protection you get at the biggest bank in the country. So an insured online bank can be just as safe for your deposits as a famous one.

[ЭКРАН: предупреждающий знак над карточкой «fintech app».]

One caution: some finance apps aren't banks themselves. They partner with banks behind the scenes. That can work fine, but make sure you understand which bank actually holds your money and how the insurance applies. If an app is vague about it, that's a red flag.

### 5:15 — How to choose one

[ЭКРАН: чек-лист из пяти пунктов.]

So how do you choose? Here's a simple checklist.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 1: «FDIC / NCUA ✓».]

One: insurance. Make sure it's FDIC or NCUA insured.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 2: «Monthly fee: $0».]

Two: no monthly fees. Fees can wipe out the extra interest.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 3: «Minimum: $0».]

Three: no or low minimum balance. You shouldn't need thousands of dollars just to open it or earn the rate.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 4: стрелки перевода между банками.]

Four: easy transfers. Check how quickly you can move money to and from your checking account. Usually it's one to a few business days.

[ЭКРАН: пункт 5: «Teaser rate» мелким шрифтом, лупа.]

Five: the real rate. Some banks advertise a high "promo" rate that drops after a few months, or only applies to small balances. Read the fine print.

[ЭКРАН: маскот сравнивает 3 карточки банков рядом.]

Comparison sites list current rates, but always confirm the rate and terms on the bank's own website.

### 6:30 — Mistakes to avoid

[ЭКРАН: три красных карточки с крестиками.]

A few mistakes to avoid.

[ЭКРАН: маскот прыгает от банка к банку.]

Chasing every tiny rate difference. Switching banks constantly for an extra 0.1% usually isn't worth the hassle.

[ЭКРАН: HYSA с надписью «retirement», крестик.]

Using a HYSA for long-term investing. It's great for an emergency fund or short-term goals, like a car or vacation in the next year or two. For retirement decades away, many people choose to invest instead, because investments have historically grown faster over long periods, although with more ups and downs.

[ЭКРАН: налоговая форма «1099-INT».]

And forgetting taxes. Interest from savings accounts is usually taxable income. You'll often get a tax form for it. Not a reason to avoid it, just something to know.

[ЭКРАН: «HYSA vs others», четыре карточки рядом.]

Quick comparison with other places to keep cash.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Checking».]

Checking accounts: great for paying bills, but usually pay little or no interest. Keep only what you need for regular spending there.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «CD».]

Certificates of deposit, or CDs: you lock your money in for a set time, like six months or a year, at a fixed rate. Good if you won't need the money and want to lock in a rate. But there's usually a penalty if you withdraw early.

[ЭКРАН: карточка «Money market».]

Money market accounts and money market funds: similar idea, often with competitive rates. Just know that money market funds at brokerages are investments, not bank deposits, so they're not FDIC insured, even though they're generally considered low risk.

[ЭКРАН: «Quick recap», карточки складываются в стопку.]

Quick recap. Many big banks pay almost nothing on savings. High-yield savings accounts, mostly at online banks, can pay several times more, with the same FDIC insurance up to the limits. Check for insurance, no fees, no minimums, easy transfers and the real rate. Rates move with the Fed, and the interest is taxable.
