## Сценарий

### 0:00 — AI is watching your wallet

[ЭКРАН: маскот и робот смотрят в длинную выписку, робот раскладывает траты по папкам.]

What if an AI watched every dollar you spend, and told you exactly where your money was going?

[ЭКРАН: график трендов поиска растёт вверх.]

AI-powered budgeting apps are one of the fastest-growing personal finance trends right now. And honestly, it makes sense. Budgeting is boring. Tracking spending is tedious. If a computer can do it for you, why not?

[ЭКРАН: замок, щит, знак вопроса.]

But it also means giving an app access to some of your most private information. So should you do it? Let's unlock it.

### 0:50 — What AI budget apps do

[ЭКРАН: схема: банк → приложение → ИИ → советы.]

First, what do these apps actually do? Most of them connect to your bank accounts and credit cards. Then they pull in your transactions automatically.

[ЭКРАН: траты сами раскладываются по категориям: food, rent, fun, subscriptions.]

AI sorts every transaction into categories: groceries, rent, restaurants, shopping, subscriptions and so on. That used to be a manual chore. Now it happens in the background.

[ЭКРАН: окно чата: «How much did I spend on food this month?» → ответ.]

Many newer apps add a chat feature. You can ask questions like, "How much did I spend on eating out this month?" or "Can I afford a $500 trip in March?" and get an answer based on your real numbers.

[ЭКРАН: уведомление «You're close to your dining budget».]

And they send alerts: when you're close to a spending limit, when a bill is due, or when something looks unusual.

### 2:15 — What they're good at

[ЭКРАН: три зелёные карточки.]

So what are AI budget tools actually good at? Three things stand out.

[ЭКРАН: подписки подсвечиваются, «forgotten?» над ними.]

One: finding forgotten subscriptions. AI is great at spotting recurring charges, like that streaming service or app you forgot you were paying for. For a lot of people, this alone pays for the app.

[ЭКРАН: график трат по месяцам, тренд вверх по одной категории.]

Two: spotting patterns. You might not notice that your food delivery spending doubled over three months. AI does. It can show trends you'd never see by glancing at your bank app.

[ЭКРАН: человек экономит время: часы ручного учёта превращаются в минуты.]

And three: saving time. No spreadsheets. No typing in every receipt. The tracking just happens. And for many people, the biggest reason budgets fail is that they stop tracking after a week.

### 3:30 — The privacy question

[ЭКРАН: тёмная сцена, папка «your financial data» под лупой.]

Now the big question: privacy and security. When you connect an app to your bank, you're giving it a detailed picture of your life. Where you shop. What you eat. Where you travel. How much you earn.

[ЭКРАН: схема: приложение → посредник по передаче данных → банк.]

Many apps connect to your bank through a third-party data company. That's normal, but it means your data passes through more than one company.

[ЭКРАН: чек-лист с вопросами.]

So before you connect anything, ask a few questions. Who owns this company? Do they sell or share your data? Do they use your data to show you ads or offers? How is it protected? Can you delete your data if you leave?

[ЭКРАН: лупа над мелким шрифтом в «Privacy Policy».]

The answers are usually in the privacy policy. It's boring, but it's worth five minutes when it's your financial life.

[ЭКРАН: замок, «2FA» на телефоне.]

And always protect the account with a strong, unique password and two-factor authentication.

### 4:45 — What AI can't do

[ЭКРАН: робот показывает график, маскот пожимает плечами и всё равно покупает.]

Here's the honest truth. AI can tell you where your money goes. It can't make you stop spending it.

[ЭКРАН: уведомление «You're over budget», палец нажимает «dismiss».]

Many people install a budgeting app, get alerts, feel guilty for a moment, and then swipe them away. The app works perfectly. The behavior doesn't change.

[ЭКРАН: ИИ даёт неверный совет, маскот поднимает бровь.]

AI can also be wrong. It might put a purchase in the wrong category, or give advice that doesn't fit your real life, because it doesn't know about your plans, your family or your goals unless you tell it. Treat its suggestions as ideas, not orders.

[ЭКРАН: маскот держит руль сам.]

The decisions are still yours.

### 5:45 — The DIY method

[ЭКРАН: «No-connect method», ноутбук с таблицей.]

Not comfortable connecting your bank to an app? There's a simple do-it-yourself method.

[ЭКРАН: выписка скачивается как файл, имена и номера счетов вымараны.]

Download last month's transactions from your bank as a file. Remove anything sensitive, like account numbers. Then paste the list into a general AI assistant and ask it to sort your spending into categories and summarize where your money went.

[ЭКРАН: ИИ рисует простую таблицу и диаграмму.]

You can ask follow-up questions like: "What are my top three spending categories?" "Which charges look like subscriptions?" "Where could I realistically cut $200 a month?"

[ЭКРАН: замок на ноутбуке, данные не уходят напрямую из банка.]

This way, nothing is connected to your bank permanently, and you decide exactly what to share. Just be thoughtful about what you paste into any online service, and check its privacy settings too.

[ЭКРАН: «Prompts that work», окно чата с примерами запросов.]

Here are a few prompts that work well for budgeting with AI.

[ЭКРАН: запрос 1 печатается.]

"Here are my monthly take-home pay and fixed bills. Build me a simple budget using the 50/30/20 rule, and tell me where I'm over."

[ЭКРАН: запрос 2.]

"Look at these transactions and list every recurring charge, with the monthly and yearly cost."

[ЭКРАН: запрос 3.]

"I want to save $1,000 for an emergency fund in six months. Suggest a realistic weekly plan based on my spending."

[ЭКРАН: запрос 4.]

"Explain the difference between paying off my credit card with the highest interest rate first versus the smallest balance first, using my numbers."

[ЭКРАН: маскот проверяет ответ ИИ калькулятором.]

And always double-check the math. AI can make arithmetic mistakes, especially with long lists of numbers. If something looks off, ask it to show its calculations.

### 7:00 — How to choose

[ЭКРАН: чек-лист выбора приложения.]

If you do want a dedicated app, here's a quick checklist.

Clear privacy policy, with no selling of your personal data.

Strong security, including two-factor authentication.

A price you're OK with. Many apps charge a monthly or yearly subscription, so make sure the money you save is bigger than the fee.

Easy to cancel and delete your data.

[ЭКРАН: пробный период, таймер.]

And use a free trial first. Give it a month and see if it actually changes your behavior. If not, cancel.

### 8:00 — Mirror, not babysitter

[ЭКРАН: маскот смотрит в зеркало-телефон, в отражении вся картина трат.]

Here's the best way to think about AI and your money. Use it as a mirror, not a babysitter.

A mirror shows you the truth: where your money is really going. That's incredibly valuable. But it's still on you to decide what to change.

[ЭКРАН: «Quick recap», карточки складываются в стопку.]

Quick recap. AI budgeting tools sort your spending, find forgotten subscriptions, spot patterns and save time. But they need access to sensitive data, so read the privacy policy and protect your account. AI can be wrong and can't change your habits for you. If you don't want to connect your bank, you can download your transactions, remove sensitive details and ask a general AI assistant to analyze them. And whichever way you go, start with a free trial and see if it actually changes what you do.
