# The Dimmers · Part 4: The Town With Lights

**Канал:** Rules of the Long Night · **Формат:** 9:16 Shorts/TikTok

## Сценарий

Six hours of power. Then the dark wins. Cole needs a better plan.

On the radio, a voice breaks through the static. Kettle Creek airstrip. We have floodlights and fuel. Anyone out there, come south.

Forty miles. In the dark. On a snowmachine with one headlight.

Ruth packs the last candles. Cole straps on the last three flares. Bear rides in the sled, ears up.

Rule four: never travel in the dark. Unless the dark is coming for you anyway.

Mile ten. The trees press in close. The headlight starts to flicker.

Mile eighteen. Something is running alongside them. They can't see it. But the snow beside the trail is being pushed down. Step. Step. Step.

Mile twenty-one. The headlight dies.

Total darkness. Cole fumbles for a flare. His gloves are too thick. Ruth screams his name.

And then the sled is empty. Bear has jumped out. He's gone, into the dark.
