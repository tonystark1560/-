# The Dimmers · Part 1: If Your Lantern Flickers With No Wind

**Канал:** Rules of the Long Night · **Формат:** 9:16 Shorts/TikTok

## Сценарий

If your lantern flickers when there's no wind… step into the light. And don't look at the dark.

Brooks Range, Alaska. November eighteenth. The sun sets at one forty-two p.m. It won't rise again for sixty-four days.

Cole has survived eleven polar nights out here. Just him, a woodstove, and Bear, a husky who's afraid of nothing.

Tonight the radio crackles. Kettle Creek, forty miles south, reports a blackout. Then another town. Then the radio goes silent.

At nine fourteen, Bear stops eating. He stares at the window. And growls. A sound Cole has never heard from him.

Cole takes the lantern out to the porch. The snow is empty. No wind. Nothing moves.

Then the lantern flickers. Once. Twice. The flame bends toward the dark, like something out there is breathing it in.

At the very edge of the light, the shadows are wrong. Too thick. And they're leaning closer.

Bear barks once. Cole fires a flare into the snow. Red light floods the yard.

The shadows snap back. Fast. Like a hand pulled from a hot stove.

In the morning dark, Cole finds tracks. Long. Thin. They circle the cabin, and stop exactly where the light ended.

Rule one: they can't cross the light. Part two: count your matches.
