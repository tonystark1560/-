# The Dimmers · Part 5: The Dog Knows

**Канал:** Rules of the Long Night · **Формат:** 9:16 Shorts/TikTok

## Сценарий

You never chase your dog into the dark. Everyone knows that. Cole does it anyway.

He lights a flare. Red light. Twenty yards of safety. Beyond it, nothing.

Then he hears it. A bark. Far away. To the left. Off the trail.

Ruth grabs his arm. That's not the way. But Bear barks again. And again. Steady. Calling.

They follow. The snow under their boots turns hard and smooth. A frozen river. And it's shining.

The moon is out. Its light bounces off the ice. Rule five: they won't cross moonlit ice. Bear knew.

Bear is waiting in the middle of the river, tail wagging. Cole has never been so happy to see a dog.

They walk the river south for hours. On both banks, the dark moves with them. Watching. Waiting.

Then the sky explodes in green. Aurora. The whole sky is glowing.

Under the northern lights, the shadows fall back. For one hour, they're safe.

Then Ruth points ahead. The lights of Kettle Creek. One by one, they're going out.
